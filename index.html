Help - Maxwell's Maniac

Goal. Sort every molecule into its matching chamber: red goes left, blue goes right. A molecule only counts when it stays on its side — shut the door behind it.

The door. The divider has one gap. Drag the gray door (or press ↑/↓) to seal the gap or open a lane. The door slides at a fixed speed: think like the demon, not like the wall.

Modes. Move — red only, all start on the wrong side. Swap — red and blue start swapped. Paint — like Swap, but molecules turn gray while on the correct side and revert if they cross back. Clear the level when every molecule sits on its side at once.

Scoring. +100 when a molecule reaches its chamber for the first time, plus 10 × seconds remaining when a level clears. When time runs out, the experiment ends.

Move / Swap / Paint modes, the sliding door and the gray-paint rule are the documented mechanics of the 1992 original. Exact scoring values, timers and speeds are this port's reconstruction.
